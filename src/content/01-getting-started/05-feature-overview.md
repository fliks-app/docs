Each row names the feature, where it lives in the app, and the page that explains it. The
conventions for **Settings > X**, **App settings > X** and **Account settings > X** are in
[Finding your way around](/getting-started/finding-your-way).

## Watching

| Feature | Where | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Play, resume, start over | Title page, **Play** | [Player overview](/player/overview) |
| Audio and subtitle tracks | Player, **Audio** and **Subtitles** buttons; title page lists | [Audio and subtitles](/player/subtitles-and-audio) |
| Subtitle appearance and timing | Player, **Subtitles**, then the gear | [Subtitles in the player](/player/subtitles-and-audio#subtitles-in-the-player) |
| Speed, quality, stats overlay | Player, **Speed** and **Settings** | [Quality and stats](/player/quality-and-stats) |
| Keyboard shortcuts, touch gestures | Player | [Keyboard and gestures](/player/keyboard-and-gestures) |
| Skip intro and credits, next episode | Player, bottom right of the picture | [Player overview](/player/overview), [Playback](/features/playback) |
| Play on another device, Chromecast | Player, **Play on another device** | [Casting and other devices](/player/casting-and-other-devices) |
| Remote control and TV pairing | User menu, **Login requests**; **App settings > Remote control** | [Pairing and remote control](/features/pairing-and-remote-control) |
| Watch parties | Home, **New party**; player, **Settings > Watch together** | [Watch party](/features/watch-party) |
| Offline downloads | Title page, **More > Download**; sidebar, **Downloads** | [Offline downloads](/features/offline-downloads) |
| Live TV | **Settings > Live TV** to add a source | [Live TV](/features/live-tv) |
