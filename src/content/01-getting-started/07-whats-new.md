> [!SUMMARY]
> The main changes of versions 7.0, 6.0, 5.2 and 5.1. The full list of every fix is on the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/fliks-app/fliks/releases) page.

## 7.0

- **Music, rebuilt.** Albums, artists and recordings have their own pages, a music search tab, a mini player and a full player, lyrics with sync and editing, and music that stays on the device. See [Music](/features/music) and [The music player](/player/music-player).
- **Karaoke.** Separate the voice from a track, sing with word-by-word lyrics, a singer queue and phone microphones, on the web, Apple TV and the Chromecast receiver. See [Lyrics and karaoke settings](/administration/lyrics-and-karaoke).
- **One account session across devices.** See what is playing on your other devices, control it, or take it over. Books and comics resume on another device too. See [Pairing and remote control](/features/pairing-and-remote-control).
- **Optimization.** Re-encode or remux library files in the background so they play without a transcode, on the server or on remote workers. See [Optimization](/administration/optimization).
- **Several download providers.** Albums can be requested and acquired with their own profile, and a title can be fetched by more than one provider. See [Download plugin](/downloads/plugin).
- **Auto-approval by genre.** Rules can now exclude genres. See [Auto-approval rules](/downloads/auto-approval).
- **Start a watch party** from the video or music player, with a programme that is the single queue. See [Watch party](/features/watch-party).
- **CarPlay audio** on iPhone and music on Apple TV. See [Mobile](/clients/mobile) and [Apple TV](/clients/apple-tv).

Version 7.0.1 is a fix release.

## 6.0

- **Single sign-on.** Sign in with an OpenID Connect provider on the web, Android and desktop apps. See [Single sign-on](/administration/single-sign-on).
- **Quick Connect with a code.** The new device shows a code and you approve it on a device already signed in. See [Quick Connect](/features/pairing-and-remote-control#pairing-a-tv-or-new-device-quick-connect).
- **Sessions you can revoke.** Each sign-in is a session that an administrator or you can end at once. See [Single sign-on](/administration/single-sign-on#sessions).
- **Books and audiobooks.** Book pages, authors, narrators and series, direct downloads, offline reading and merged multi-file audiobooks. See [Books and audiobooks](/features/books-and-audiobooks).
- **Comics.** Comic volumes with an image reader, ComicInfo.xml support and missing issues acquired automatically. See [Comics](/features/comics).
- **Metadata provider plugins** are bundled with the server. See [Built-in plugins](/built-in-plugins/overview).

## 5.2

- **Watch parties.** Synced playback, chat, voice, film proposals and votes, and a programme per party. See [Watch party](/features/watch-party).
- **First admin from a setup page** instead of a default password, and a choice of who sees the login user list. See [Users and permissions](/administration/users-and-permissions#login-user-list).
- **Chromecast receiver polish.** Progress bar with intro and credits bands, skip cues, and subtitle style and margin edited live from the player. See [Chromecast](/clients/chromecast).
- **Desktop app integration.** Media keys and system controls on Windows, macOS and Linux, native Wayland, an APT repository and a Snap. See [Desktop](/clients/desktop).
- **Surround sound on Android TV.** AC3, E-AC3, DTS and TrueHD pass through to your AV receiver. See [Android TV](/clients/android-tv#surround-sound-through-an-av-receiver).
- **Styled subtitles** (ASS and SSA) are burned in on clients that cannot draw them. See [Burned-in subtitles](/features/subtitles#burned-in-subtitles).

## 5.1

- **Library cleanup.** Rules that recommend titles to remove, with a leaving-soon list, play history criteria and reminders. See [Library cleanup](/administration/library-cleanup).
- **Better offline downloads.** Download a whole season or show, group by show, pause and resume, Wi-Fi only, and keep the next episodes on the device. See [Offline downloads](/features/offline-downloads).
- **macOS server app** updates itself in place. See [macOS](/install/macos).
