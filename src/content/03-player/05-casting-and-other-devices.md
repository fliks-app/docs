> [!SUMMARY]
> Press the cast icon at the top right of the player and pick a Chromecast or another Fliks screen signed in to your account. Choose **This device** to bring playback back.

## Play on a TV, Chromecast or another screen

The button with the cast icon, at the top right of the player, lists every screen that can play
what you are watching.

![The Play on another device list: This device, Chromecast, and another browser signed in to the account](/img/player-menu-devices.webp)

| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| **This device** | Plays here. The entry that is highlighted is where the video is playing now. |
| **Chromecast...** | Opens the browser's own picker of Chromecasts on your network. |
| A device name (for example a phone or a TV) | Another Fliks screen signed in to your account. Pick it and the playback moves there. |

The same icon is in the top bar of the title pages, so you can choose the screen before pressing
play. On a phone, the list opens as a sheet from the bottom.

## What happens when you pick a screen

- **Casting to a Chromecast.** The server prepares a version the Chromecast can play, and the
  player turns into a remote control: the bar acts on the TV. Quality, audio and subtitles are
  chosen on your side. Settings for HDR, maximum quality and audio channels are in
  **App settings > Chromecast**. See [Chromecast](/clients/chromecast).
- **Another Fliks screen.** The title opens on that device, and your player controls it: play,
  pause, seek, volume, next episode, quality, audio and subtitle tracks. A chip with the device
  name shows where it plays. See [Pairing and remote control](/features/pairing-and-remote-control).

To bring the playback back, open the list again and choose **This device**.

> [!NOTE]
> The subtitle synchronize panel is not offered while casting. A TV app cannot cast: it can only
> be a target of the other screens.
