> [!SUMMARY]
> `Space` plays or pauses, `Left`/`Right` jump 10 seconds, `f` is fullscreen. Phone gestures and TV remote keys are listed below.

## Keyboard shortcuts

These work in the web player while the picture has the focus, not while you type in a field or
while a menu or dialog is open.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Space` or `k` | Play or pause |
| `Left` / `Right` | Go back or forward 10 seconds |
| `j` / `l` | Go back or forward 30 seconds |
| `,` / `.` | Pause, then step one frame back or forward |
| `<` / `>` | Slow down or speed up by 0.25x (from 0.25x to 2x) |
| `m` | Mute or unmute |
| `f` | Enter or leave fullscreen |
| `p` | Picture-in-picture |
| `Escape` | Hide the controls if they are showing, otherwise leave the player |
| `Up` | Show the controls (when hidden) |
| `Home` / `End` | Jump to the start or the end, when the seek bar has the focus |

The seek, play and frame keys do nothing for a guest of a [watch party](/features/watch-party)
that only the host controls.

:::details How the keyboard seek bar scrubs
With the controls hidden, `Left` and `Right` raise a thin seek bar and scrub from there. Tap the
key to move 10 seconds. Hold it and the step grows: 10 seconds, then 30, then 60, then 5 minutes.
The jump happens when you let go. A scrub that lands within 5 seconds of a chapter mark snaps to
it. Pressing `Left` or `Right` the first time on a resting bar only arms it, so a stray key never
moves you.
:::

:::details Move between the controls with the keyboard
Once a button of the bar has the focus, the arrow keys move between them and `Enter` presses one. On a
hidden bar, `Enter` shows the bar first instead of pressing a button. The **Skip intro** and
**Next episode** buttons take the focus when they appear, so `Enter` presses them at once.
:::

## Touch gestures on a phone

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tap the picture | Show or hide the controls |
| Tap the big arrows | Go back or forward 10 seconds |
| Tap the big middle button | Play or pause |
| Drag the seek bar | Scrub through the video, with a preview frame |
| Drag a menu sheet down | Close it |

During a [watch party](/features/watch-party) with reactions on, a swipe up from the bottom right
corner opens the reaction row.

There is no double-tap to skip: use the **Rewind 10 seconds** and **Forward 10 seconds**
buttons.

## TV remotes

On a TV (Android TV, Samsung, LG) the player follows the remote.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Up` | Show the controls |
| `Left` / `Right` | Scrub, as on the keyboard |
| `OK` | Show the controls (when hidden), or press the focused button |
| Play / Pause | Play or pause |
| Fast forward / Rewind | Forward or back 10 seconds |
| Stop | Leave the player |
| Back | Leave the player |

Any other key, when the controls are hidden, wakes them. The controls stay up for five seconds on
a TV, three on other devices. Focus lands on the **Play** button each time the bar comes back,
so `OK` pauses.

The Apple TV app uses the Siri Remote and its own native controls. See [Apple TV](/clients/apple-tv).

## Music

The music player has no letter shortcuts. It answers the media keys of a keyboard, a headset or a TV remote: **Play/Pause**, **Play**, **Pause**, **Stop** (closes the player), **Next track** and **Previous track**. The same actions come from the system's media controls, which also seek. See [The music player](/player/music-player).

On a phone, swipe the title of the mini player left or right to change track, swipe a queue row sideways to remove it, and drag its handle to reorder.
