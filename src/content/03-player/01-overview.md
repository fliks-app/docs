> [!SUMMARY]
> Open a title and press **Play**. Move the mouse or tap to show the controls; `f` goes fullscreen. Seek bar, skip intro, picture-in-picture and the phone layout are below.

Playing music? It has its own player: see [The music player](/player/music-player).

## Start playing a title

Open a title from your library, then press the **Play** button on its page. The same button reads
**Resume at 5:05** when you stopped part way through, and a **From the beginning** button appears
next to it.

![The title page of a partly watched movie: Resume at 5:05, From the beginning, then the watched, download, like, add-to-list and More buttons](/img/player-detail-page.webp)

The line above the buttons shows what will play: **Video**, **Audio** and **Subtitles**. Each is a
choice you can change before pressing play:

- **Video** becomes a list when the title has several files (two encodes of the same film, for
  example). Pick the file you want.
- **Audio** and **Subtitles** open a list of tracks. See
  [Audio and subtitles](/player/subtitles-and-audio).

For a series, the buttons act on the episode shown: open another one to play it.

## The control bar

Move the mouse, tap the screen, or press a key to show the controls. They hide on their own during playback.

![The player with numbered markers on every control](/img/player-overview-annotated.webp)

:::details What each numbered control does
| # | Control | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Back** arrow | Leaves the player and returns to the page you came from. |
| 2 | Title logo | The logo of what is playing. The title and episode name are at the bottom left. |
| 3 | **Volume** slider | Sets the volume. |
| 4 | **Volume** button | Mutes and unmutes. |
| 5 | **Picture-in-Picture** | Moves the video to a small floating window. |
| 6 | **Play on another device** | Casts or hands the playback over to another screen. See [Casting and other devices](/player/casting-and-other-devices). |
| 7 | Seek bar | Shows your position. Click or drag to jump. The times below it show the time played, the time left and the total length. |
| 8 | **Rewind 10 seconds** | Jumps back 10 seconds. |
| 9 | **Play** / **Pause** | Starts or pauses. |
| 10 | **Forward 10 seconds** | Jumps forward 10 seconds. |
| 11 | **Subtitles** | Picks a subtitle track and opens the appearance and sync options. Only shown when the file has subtitles. |
| 12 | **Speed** (`1x`) | Plays slower or faster, from 0.25x to 2x. |
| 13 | **Settings** | Quality, fill screen, stats, and a shortcut back to the title page. |
| 14 | **Fullscreen** | Fills the screen. Press it again to leave. |
:::

Two more buttons appear only when they apply:

- **Audio** (a headphones icon, next to **Subtitles**) when the file has more than one audio track.
- **Next episode** (a skip-forward icon, left of **Subtitles**) when something is queued after
  the current item: the next episode, the next item of a playlist.

Clicking the picture itself pauses and resumes. Double-clicking toggles fullscreen.

### The settings menu

The gear opens a short menu.

![The Settings menu: Quality, Fill screen, Stats for nerds, Watch together and Open media](/img/player-menu-settings.webp)

- **Quality** opens the quality list. See [Quality and stats](/player/quality-and-stats).
- **Queue** appears when several items are queued (a playlist, a series): jump to any of them.
- **Fill screen** crops the picture to fill the window instead of leaving black bars.
- **Stats for nerds** shows the technical overlay.
- **Watch together** starts a [watch party](/features/watch-party).
- **Open media** goes to the title page.

The bar, the subtitle list, then the appearance options:

![An animation: the Subtitles button is pressed, the track list opens, then the Appearance options](/img/player-subtitles-menu-open.webp)

## Seek bar previews

Point at the seek bar to see a small preview frame, the time under the pointer and, when the file
has chapters, the chapter name. Click to jump there, or press and drag to scrub.

![Hovering the seek bar: a preview frame with the time 8:08 and the chapter name](/img/player-seek-preview.webp)

Thin marks on the bar are chapters, when the file has them. Previews missing? See
[Scrubbing previews](/features/playback#scrubbing-previews).

## Skip intro and next episode

On a series, two buttons appear in the bottom right corner of the picture while they apply:

- **Skip intro** while the episode is in its opening titles.
- **Next episode** near the end, during the credits, when another episode is queued.

Each goes away after a short countdown if you ignore it. With a keyboard or remote, Enter or OK presses it.

**Skip intro automatically** and **Automatically play next episode** in **App settings > Playback** do it for you.
How intros are detected: [Playback](/features/playback#intro-and-credits-detection).

## Picture-in-picture and fullscreen

**Picture-in-Picture**, top right, pops the video out into a floating window on top of other windows. Press it again
to bring the video back.

**Fullscreen**, bottom right, fills the screen (`f`, or double-click the picture). On a phone, **Lock orientation**
keeps the screen from rotating.

## On a phone

The same controls are laid out for touch. Tap the picture to show them. Big **Rewind 10 seconds**,
**Play**/**Pause** and **Forward 10 seconds** buttons sit in the middle, and the row above the
seek bar holds **Subtitles**, **Audio**, **Speed**, **Settings** and **Fullscreen**. Each menu
opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

![The player on a phone: back, picture-in-picture and cast at the top, big transport buttons in the middle, subtitles, speed, settings and fullscreen above the seek bar](/img/player-phone-controls.webp)

See [Keyboard and gestures](/player/keyboard-and-gestures) for every shortcut.
