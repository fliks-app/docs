> [!SUMMARY]
> Open your server's address in a browser and sign in. Your browser can install it as an app with its own icon and window.

## Sign in

Open `http://<host>:4848` in any current browser and pick your account (or **Enter another user**).
There is nothing to install.

![The web app's login screen: your accounts, Quick Connect and Enter another user](/img/clients-web-login.webp)

## Install it as an app

Use your browser's **Install** option (address bar icon, or **Add to Home Screen** on a phone). Fliks
then opens in its own window, no address bar.

## Browser notes

:::details Browser-specific notes
- **iOS Safari** uses its own native fullscreen video player instead of Fliks' fullscreen controls.
- **Casting**: the Chromecast button only appears in browsers with Google's Cast extension (built
  into Chrome).
- **Picture-in-picture** uses the browser's own support, where it offers it.
- **Downloads**: a browser stores offline downloads in its own storage, only for titles you pick. **Automatic download** on a playlist is not available. See [Offline downloads](/features/offline-downloads).
:::

The web app updates itself: after a server update the page reloads on its own.
