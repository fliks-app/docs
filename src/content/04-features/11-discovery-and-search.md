> [!SUMMARY]
> Use **Search** in the sidebar to find titles in and outside your library, and **App settings > Home** to show, hide and reorder the home rows. **Account settings > Spoilers** hides what you haven't watched yet.

## The home page

![The Fliks home page with its Continue Watching and Recently Added rows](/img/home.webp)

The home page is built from rows you can show, hide and reorder yourself, from **App settings >
Home** (this is remembered per device, not per account). You can also click the **Customize home** tile at the very bottom of the home page.

:::details What each home row shows
| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Watch parties | Parties planned or running, with a **New party** button, see [Watch party](/features/watch-party) |
| Member recommendations | Titles other users sent you directly, see [Sharing and social](/features/sharing-and-social) |
| Libraries | One card per library you can access |
| Continue watching | Movies you're partway through, plus the next episode to watch for each series you've started |
| Recommendations | Personalised suggestions based on your own watch history, see below |
| My favorites | Titles, seasons and episodes you've liked |
| Recently added | The newest arrivals across your libraries |
| Recent playlists | Your own and shared playlists |
| Coming soon | What's releasing or airing soon, from the calendar below |
:::

If you're allowed to make [Requests](/downloads/requests), a **Recent requests** row is added too,
shown by default above Recently added (not on TVs). Each library also gets its own "Recently added"
row, hidden by default, that you can turn on alongside the combined one.

![The App settings > Home page, with its reorderable list of home rows and a switch for each](/img/app-settings-home.webp)

Drag a row by its handle (or use the arrows) to reorder it, flip its switch to hide it, and click
**Reset** to go back to the default layout. Two settings sit under the list:

- **Content of "Recently added"** picks what feeds every "Recently added" row: recently added
  media, recently added files, or both.
- **Only show media I requested** limits **Recently added** and **Coming soon** to what you
  requested.

The gear on the **Libraries** row opens a dialog to reorder your libraries and hide the ones you
don't want on the home page and in the sidebar.

![The Libraries dialog opened from the gear of the Libraries row](/img/app-settings-home-libraries.webp)

## Search your library and beyond

Click **Search** in the sidebar and type. The tabs are **All**, **Video**, **Books**, **Music**
and **Members** (to find other members by name; hidden on TVs and if you've turned off sharing).
Results are split into **In your library** and **Not in your library**.

![A search for a title in the library, with the outside results below](/img/search-results.webp)

The **Search outside the library** switch, beside **Not in your library**, also searches your
metadata provider (TMDB or TVDB, whichever is preferred, falling back to the other when it finds
nothing), so a title you don't have yet still turns up, ready to add or request.

With nothing typed, the page shows suggestions from your library. With external search on, it
adds trending titles (**Today** or **This week**) and popular ones.

![Browsing trending and popular titles from the search page](/img/discover.webp)

**Filters** narrow the results by sort order, genre, minimum rating and release year (genres need
external search on).

## Calendar

The **Calendar** page lays out a month at a time: when a movie in your library is due in cinemas,
digitally, or physically, and when each episode of a series you have is airing. Only titles already
in one of your libraries appear here; it doesn't suggest anything new. Toggle any of the four
categories off if you'd rather not see it.

## Spoiler protection

**Account settings > Spoilers** lets you avoid seeing what you haven't gotten to yet, with one
master switch (**Hide spoiler information**) and three things it can hide once it's on:

:::details What each spoiler setting hides
| Setting | What it hides |
|---|---|
| Blur thumbnails | The still of an unwatched episode, and its page's backdrop |
| Blur synopses | The plot summary of an unwatched film, season or episode |
| Hide episode names | Shows "Episode 3" instead of a title that gives away the plot |
:::

![The Account settings > Spoilers page with its Hide spoiler information switch](/img/account-spoilers.webp)

Clicking a masked item reveals it for the moment; marking it as watched unmasks it for good. Each
of the three is its own toggle, and the setting follows your account onto every device, since it's
stored on your profile rather than the device you're using.

## Recommended for you

The **Recommendations** row on the home page (and the **Suggestions** view inside a library) is
built from what you've actually watched, not a fixed list: it looks at the genres of the titles
you've recently finished, weighted toward the most recent ones, and surfaces library titles you
haven't started yet that share those genres. Don't want to see something suggested again? Dismiss
it from the home page row, and it stays hidden until you reset your dismissed list from **Account
settings > Recommendations**.

It only draws on genres, and starts once you have finished a title that has genres.

This personalised row is separate from the recommendations members send each other directly, and
from the "popular among people you follow" row built from your followed users' own watch history:
both of those are covered in [Sharing and social](/features/sharing-and-social).
